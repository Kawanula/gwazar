Zomba Digital Literacy Study

Abstract: Investigating the extent of mobile technology use for homework and associated digital literacy levels among secondary school students in Zomba District, Malawi.

Key Finding: 60.0% of the 460 students surveyed rely on smartphones for approximately 80% of their academic tasks, using tools like ChatGPT and Gemini. Rural users face high costs and depend on discounted TNM night bundles.
